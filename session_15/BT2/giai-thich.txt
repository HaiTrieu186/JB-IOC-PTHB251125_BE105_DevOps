Nguyen nhan: scrape_interval va evaluation_interval dang de 1s.
- scrape_interval: bao lau Prometheus cao metrics 1 lan. De 1s thi moi giay goi backend:8080 mot lan, nhieu du lieu gap 15 lan, ton CPU, RAM, o dia, backend cung bi goi lien tuc.
- evaluation_interval: bao lau Prometheus danh gia rule (alert, recording rule) 1 lan. De 1s la vo ich vi du lieu moi chi co sau moi lan scrape.

Cac buoc sua:
1. Mo prometheus.yml, sua phan global:
   scrape_interval: 1s  ->  15s
   evaluation_interval: 1s  ->  15s
2. Reload cau hinh (khong can restart container, vi compose co --web.enable-lifecycle):
   curl -X POST http://localhost:9090/-/reload
   Hoac restart: docker compose restart prometheus
3. Kiem tra: vao http://localhost:9090/config thay scrape_interval: 15s la da ap dung.

Ket qua: Prometheus nhe hon ro ret. Grafana lay du lieu tu Prometheus chu khong goi truc tiep app nen bieu do van lien mach, chi la moi 15 giay co 1 diem du lieu.

Luu y: khi dung rate() thi cua so thoi gian nen tu [1m] tro len (it nhat gap 4 lan scrape_interval), khong dung [15s].
