Cac buoc lam:
1. Them service node-exporter vao docker-compose.yml va them job node-exporter vao prometheus.yml (target node-exporter:9100), chay docker compose up -d.
2. Vao Grafana (localhost:3000) > Dashboards > New > New dashboard > Add visualization > chon data source Prometheus.

Bieu do 1 - Time series (CPU %):
Query: 100 - (avg(rate(node_cpu_seconds_total{mode="idle"}[5m])) * 100)
Giai thich: node_cpu_seconds_total la bo dem tang dan nen dung rate() de tinh toc do. Lay mode idle (CPU ranh), tinh trung binh cac core, lay 100 tru di ra % CPU dang dung.
Unit: Standard options > Unit > Percent (0-100).

Bieu do 2 - Stat (RAM con trong):
Query: node_memory_MemFree_bytes
Chon loai bieu do Stat de hien 1 con so lon.

Bay don vi (Bytes -> MB/GB):
Khong can nhan chia trong query. Chi can vao Standard options > Unit > chon bytes(IEC) hoac bytes(SI) (nhom Data), Grafana tu doi sang MB hoac GB cho dep.
Khong chon unit "megabytes" vi unit do hieu gia tri da la MB roi, se hien sai.
