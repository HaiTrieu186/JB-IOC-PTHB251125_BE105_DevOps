Cach import dashboard bang ID:
1. Vao Grafana (localhost:3000) > Dashboards > New > Import.
2. Nhap ID 4701 vao o "Import via grafana.com", bam Load.
3. Chon data source Prometheus, bam Import.

3 bieu do quan trong nhat:
1. JVM Heap: vung nho chua object cua app. Neu duong "used" cu tang ma khong giam sau moi lan don thi la memory leak, cuoi cung app bi OutOfMemoryError.
2. Garbage Collection (GC time/pause): JVM tu don object khong dung. GC chay nhieu hoac pause lau thi app bi cham, dung hinh. GC time cao thuong di kem Heap gan day.
3. Threads: so luong thread dang chay. Tang lien tuc la co the bi ro thread hoac thread bi ket (deadlock, cho DB), gay ton RAM va cham app.

Loi "No data" va cach sua:
Dashboard 4701 co bien application o dau trang, moi bieu do deu loc theo bien nay. App khong gan label application thi bien rong nen bao No data.
Sua trong application.yml, them:

management:
  metrics:
    tags:
      application: ${spring.application.name}

Dong nay gan label application="ecommerce-api" vao moi metric nen bien co gia tri va bieu do hien du lieu.

Can them dependency trong pom.xml: spring-boot-starter-actuator va micrometer-registry-prometheus. Bat endpoint prometheus (management.endpoints.web.exposure.include) va them job ecommerce-api voi metrics_path /actuator/prometheus vao prometheus.yml.
