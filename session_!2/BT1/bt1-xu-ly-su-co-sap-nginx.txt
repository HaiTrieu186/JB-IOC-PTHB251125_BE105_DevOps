Bai tap 1 - Xu ly su co sap Nginx

1. Nguyen nhan loi

Dong "proxy_pass http://localhost:8080" bi THIEU dau cham phay (;) o cuoi dong. Moi chi thi (directive) trong Nginx bat buoc phai ket thuc bang dau ;, neu thieu, Nginx se hieu dong tiep theo (proxy_set_header Host $host;) la mot phan cua chinh dong proxy_pass, lam sai lech cu phap. Vi vay Nginx bao loi "unexpected proxy_set_header" ngay tai dong 7, du ban chat loi that su nam o dong 6 (thieu dau ;).

2. Cau hinh sau khi sua

server {
    listen 80;
    server_name example.com;

    location / {
        proxy_pass http://localhost:8080;
        proxy_set_header Host $host;
        proxy_set_header X-Real-IP $remote_addr;
    }
}

3. Lenh kiem tra va reload

Kiem tra cu phap TRUOC khi ap dung, khong lam gian doan service dang chay:
sudo nginx -t

Neu ket qua "syntax is ok" va "test is successful" thi ap dung bang reload (khong dung restart de tranh gian doan ket noi dang online):
sudo systemctl reload nginx
